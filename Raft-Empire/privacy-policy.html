Privacy Policy for Raft Empire - Build & Expand

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Raft Empire - Build & Expand, published by Maialas Games, handles your information.

Maialas Games is Samuele Cusi, an independent game developer based in Italy, and is the data controller for the purposes of this Policy.

By downloading or using the game you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the game.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect, store or transmit any of the following to our own servers: your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, photos, contacts, location, microphone or camera data. Maialas Games operates no servers and receives no personal data from you directly.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (such as your raft level, buildings, resources and settings) in a file stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and is not backed up to any online service by the game. Uninstalling the game deletes this data permanently.

3. Advertising

The game displays advertising provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC ("Google"). Advertising is how the game is funded and it is available free of charge because of it.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate, non-precise location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • information about your interactions with the ads shown (for example, whether an ad was viewed or clicked).

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller, under its own terms. For details on what Google collects and why, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your consent choices (EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open the game you are shown a consent message that asks whether you agree to the use of your data for personalised advertising. This message is provided through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

  • If you consent, you may be shown personalised ads based on your interests.
  • If you do not consent, you will still see advertising, but it will be non-personalised: it is based only on general context and not on your interests.

Your choice is stored on your device and you can change it at any time from the privacy options available in the game's settings screen.

5. How to limit ad tracking

Independently of the game, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time from your device settings: on Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by manufacturer and Android version). Deleting your advertising ID prevents the use of that identifier for ad personalisation.

6. Children's privacy

The game is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate action.

7. Legal basis and your rights

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, personalised advertising is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time as described in section 4. Non-personalised advertising is based on the legitimate interest in funding the game.

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because Maialas Games does not hold any personal data about you, requests concerning data processed for advertising should be directed to Google using the contact details in the Google Privacy Policy. We are happy to help you with such a request if you contact us first.

8. Data retention

We retain no data, because we collect none. game progress stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game. Retention of advertising data is governed by Google's own retention policies.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy, for example if the game adds new features or services. The date at the top of this page always shows the version currently in force. Significant changes will be reflected in the game's store listing.

10. Contact

For any question about this Privacy Policy or about privacy in the game, write to: MaialasGames@hotmail.com
